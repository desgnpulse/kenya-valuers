# Draft Statement of Work: Phase 0 Discovery

**Client:** Kenya Valuers & Estate Agents  
**Supplier:** DesgnPulse  
**Status:** Discussion draft only. No commercial commitment exists until both parties approve a final version.  
**Prepared:** 28 July 2026

## 1. Purpose

Phase 0 will determine the smallest viable, evidence-based digital platform scope for KV. It is a discovery engagement, not a commitment to build a full portal, marketplace, valuation model or mobile application.

The engagement will convert business priorities, inventory reality, lead operations and compliance constraints into a build-ready Phase-1 decision.

## 2. Objectives

- Confirm the primary business outcome and priority audiences.
- Establish a reliable picture of listing data, media, lifecycle and migration constraints.
- Map property, callback and professional-service lead journeys from capture to ownership and reporting.
- Validate the core mobile and desktop user journeys with KV stakeholders.
- Define the requirements for privacy, content approvals, accessibility, SEO, analytics, integrations and launch governance.
- Recommend a phased implementation scope, architecture direction, estimate range and delivery plan.

## 3. Scope of work

### 3.1 Kick-off and stakeholder alignment

- Confirm executive sponsor, operational product owner, content owner, data/technical owner and valuation/compliance reviewer.
- Hold a kick-off session to confirm success measures, decision cadence, working methods, confidentiality requirements and risk register.
- Produce a short project charter and decision log.

### 3.2 Inventory and content audit

- Review a client-approved, read-only non-production sample of active and recently inactive listings.
- Profile property identifiers, categories, status, location fields, prices, media, descriptions, agents and data-quality issues.
- Map the current publication, amendment, withdrawal and archival process.
- Define a proposed canonical listing model, validation rules and migration assumptions.

### 3.3 Lead and service workflow audit

- Map current property enquiries, WhatsApp/callback requests and valuation/service requests.
- Identify lead sources, routing, ownership, response expectations, escalation and outcome recording.
- Define a minimum lead data model and baseline reporting requirements.
- Identify the workflow boundary between a service request and a professional valuation instruction.

### 3.4 Experience and platform definition

- Create prioritised information architecture and user journeys for public discovery, listing detail, property enquiry and professional-service request.
- Produce reviewable mobile and desktop prototypes for the highest-value journeys.
- Define content, media, location-disclosure and credential-claim policies needed for launch.
- Document phase gates for maps, accounts, price intelligence, automated estimates, marketplace capabilities and native applications.

### 3.5 Technical, risk and launch planning

- Compare practical architecture options against data, performance, security, support and total-cost needs.
- Define migration, redirect, analytics, privacy, accessibility, testing, staging, backup and rollback requirements.
- Produce a Phase-1 backlog with acceptance criteria, assumptions, exclusions, dependencies and delivery sequence.

## 4. Deliverables

1. Project charter, stakeholder map and decision log.
2. Inventory/data audit and proposed canonical listing schema.
3. Lead and professional-service workflow map.
4. Information architecture and validated priority prototypes.
5. Requirements pack covering functional, content, privacy, accessibility, SEO, analytics and operational needs.
6. Architecture decision record and integration/vendor decision framework.
7. Migration and redirect approach with launch risks.
8. Phased Phase-1 scope, estimate range, timeline and acceptance criteria.
9. Executive readout with recommendation and decision options.

## 5. Exclusions

Unless added by a signed change request, Phase 0 excludes:

- Production development, deployment, domain/DNS changes or live data migration.
- Paid vendor procurement, licence purchase, analytics/CRM setup or third-party integration.
- Formal legal, tax, valuation, financial, regulatory or data-protection advice.
- Automated valuation model development, public property index publication, user accounts, native apps, open marketplace features or payment flows.
- Access to title documents, identity documents, valuation reports, bank data or any other sensitive client/customer records.

## 6. Client responsibilities and approvals

KV will:

- Appoint a CEO sponsor and named operational owners with authority to make timely decisions.
- Provide approved access to a non-production inventory sample, agreed analytics exports and relevant policies only after confidentiality and privacy conditions are agreed.
- Confirm source-of-truth rules for listings, content, credentials, service wording and lead handling.
- Attend agreed workshops and return consolidated feedback within the agreed decision window.
- Approve any expansion to data access, paid vendors, integrations or production work in writing.

DesgnPulse will:

- Keep client materials confidential and use them solely for the approved discovery purpose.
- Minimise requested data and avoid sensitive documents by default.
- Maintain a decision and risk log; flag assumptions that could alter scope, cost or delivery.
- Deliver reviewable artifacts and seek approval before moving to a consequential next step.

## 7. Indicative cadence

The anticipated discovery period is **three to five weeks** after kick-off and receipt of agreed inputs. Final dates depend on stakeholder availability, data readiness and review turnaround.

| Week | Focus | Client decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Kick-off, priorities, access boundary, current-state audit | Charter and owner confirmation |
| 2 | Inventory and lead workflow | Data and operating-rule validation |
| 3 | Journeys, information architecture and prototype review | Priority-journey approval |
| 4 | Architecture, migration, requirements and Phase-1 plan | Scope and option review |
| 5, if needed | Executive readout and commercial decision | Proceed, revise or pause |

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Phase 0 is complete when KV has received and reviewed all deliverables, and the executive sponsor can make an informed decision to proceed, revise or pause based on:

- A named target outcome and measurable baseline plan.
- An approved priority journey set and operational ownership model.
- A documented inventory/migration feasibility assessment.
- A clear boundary for valuation, privacy and client data.
- A Phase-1 scope with risks, assumptions, dependencies, acceptance criteria and commercial options.
