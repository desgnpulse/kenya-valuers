# Internal Financial Scenario Notes

**Status:** Internal planning exercise. Not a quote, budget, estimate, promise or client-facing document.  
**Purpose:** Retain the useful financial framing from an alternate research pass without converting unvalidated assumptions into a commercial commitment.

## What the exercise suggests

The alternate pass modelled a broad, four-month Phase-1 programme at **KES 2.8M–4.5M**, with later phases increasing the total programme size materially. This is useful as a discussion compass: a full platform with migration, maps, accounts, CRM, messaging and operational reporting is a substantial engagement, not a simple website refresh.

## Why it is not ready to quote

The range has not been reconciled with:

- Actual inventory count, data quality, media migration and legacy-platform export constraints.
- KV's lead-routing process, staff capacity, CRM choice and response service level.
- The exact scope of accounts, mapping, messaging, vendor management and reporting.
- Client privacy, compliance, hosting, support, procurement and acceptance requirements.
- A selected delivery team, implementation stack, timeline or change-control model.

Those unknowns could materially move cost and duration. The Phase-0 discovery exists to turn them into a priced, testable scope.

## Meeting use

Do not volunteer a price range as the meeting ask. If Michael asks about investment level, explain that the intended first release is a meaningful platform programme rather than a cosmetic redesign, and offer a fixed Phase-1 proposal only after Phase 0 validates data, operations and scope.

## Guardrail

No financial number from this note may be copied into a client document, invoice, proposal or conversation as a commitment without Jay's explicit approval and a signed scope.
