# Internal Phase-0 Negotiation Card

**Status:** Internal preparation only. Do not send as a proposal or treat any term as agreed.  
**Meeting objective:** Secure a bounded paid discovery engagement, not negotiate a full build before KV's operational facts are known.

## Anchor

| Item | Recommended position |
| --- | --- |
| Phase-0 fee | **KES 450,000**, exclusive of applicable taxes |
| Engagement type | Fixed fee for the defined discovery deliverables |
| Duration | Three to five weeks after kick-off and agreed inputs |
| Scope | The Phase-0 SOW only; no build, migration, integration, vendor spend or production work |
| Outcome | A decision-ready Phase-1 scope, cost, risk and implementation plan |

## Recommended payment structure

**Position to propose:** 50% on acceptance of the signed Phase-0 SOW; 50% on delivery of the executive readout and Phase-1 decision pack.

Why this is balanced:

- It gives KV a defined second payment tied to a tangible decision package.
- It covers the up-front audit, workshops, prototype and analysis effort.
- It avoids a time-and-materials model that would weaken the fixed-scope proposition.

Do not alter the payment structure in the SOW until Jay approves it for client use.

## Scope guardrails

The fixed fee includes only the nine deliverables in the Phase-0 SOW. It does not include:

- Building or launching the public site or staff console.
- Cleaning, migrating or manually enriching the full inventory.
- Paid vendor selection, licences, implementation or third-party account setup.
- User research recruitment, travel, photography, legal advice or specialist compliance review.
- Formal valuation work, AVM development, index publication or document-portal design.

If KV asks for one of these, state that it can be assessed as a Phase-1 component or a written change request, after its dependency and cost are clear.

## Concessions that preserve value

| If KV asks | Possible response | Do not concede |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the fee be reduced? | Keep scope intact and offer the 50/50 structure first. If necessary, reduce workshop/prototype breadth, not the core audit and decision pack. | A lower fee while retaining the full scope. |
| Can you start with an unpaid audit? | A short pre-sales discussion is already complete. The next work involves client data, workshops and decision artifacts, so it is paid discovery. | Data audit or prototypes for free. |
| Can you quote Phase 1 now? | Give the likely programme direction and explain that a fixed quote follows the inventory, lead-workflow and architecture decisions. | A fixed build price before discovery. |
| Can we add an AVM or app now? | Record it as a roadmap option with a separate evidence and governance gate. | Folding it into Phase 0 or the initial build promise. |

## Approval sequence to seek

1. Michael sponsors Phase 0.
2. KV names its operational owners and agrees the data-access boundary.
3. Both parties approve the SOW, fee, payment terms and start date.
4. KV provides the agreed inputs and workshops begin.
5. The executive readout produces a proceed, revise or pause decision for Phase 1.

## Close

The fee purchases certainty before KV makes the larger platform investment. If the work confirms that the initiative should pause or change direction, that is still a successful Phase-0 outcome.
