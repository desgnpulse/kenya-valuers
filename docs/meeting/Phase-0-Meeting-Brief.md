# Kenya Valuers: Phase-0 CEO Meeting Brief

**Audience:** Michael Otieno, CEO, Kenya Valuers & Estate Agents
**Purpose:** Decide whether KV should commission a bounded Phase-0 discovery.
**Status:** Internal preparation only; not a proposal, quote or client commitment.

## Outcome to seek

End with one explicit outcome and a named next action:

1. **Proceed:** Michael sponsors Phase 0; KV names its operating owners, data-access boundary and commercial route.
2. **Revise:** KV has a different priority or constraint; DesgnPulse returns a revised scope before any commitment.
3. **Pause:** Record the blocking dependency, owner and revisit date.

## Opening and market context

“We have narrowed the opportunity to one testable question: how KV can turn premium-property and professional-services credibility into more qualified, measurable enquiries. We propose validating the operating reality before either side commits to a platform build.”

HassConsult remains the established private benchmark. BuyRentKenya launched its first index in July 2025, before the earlier KV proposal. KNBS launched the official Residential Property Price Index in April 2026. The implication is not a rushed KV index: any future insight product needs clear methodology, governed data and a role that complements the public benchmark.

## Questions to resolve

- What specific outcome makes this worthwhile within 12 months?
- Which audiences, locations and property/services categories must win first, and what must not be promoted?
- Where do listings originate, who controls live/changed/withdrawn status, and what data or media problems exist?
- How are property, callback and valuation requests currently received, assigned, followed up and reported?
- What response standard can KV realistically own?
- Who approves listing facts, price/location disclosure, credentials and valuation-service wording?
- Will Michael sponsor Phase 0 and appoint product, data, content, lead-operations and valuation/compliance owners?
- May DesgnPulse inspect only an approved read-only, non-production listing sample and agreed analytics exports?
- What confidentiality, privacy, procurement, budget and sign-off conditions apply?

## Phase-0 offer and negotiation stance

**Objective:** a decision-ready Phase-1 scope, not a speculative feature list.

**Included:** approved sample inventory/media audit; property and professional-service lead-flow map; analytics/SEO baseline; priority mobile/desktop journey prototypes; content/privacy/migration/redirect/acceptance requirements; Corviat compatibility decision; phased scope, risks, delivery plan and estimate.

**Proposed commercial anchor:** KES 450,000 exclusive of applicable taxes, fixed for this scope, with 50% on a signed Phase-0 SOW and 50% on executive readout. Do not alter the payment structure or state a Phase-1 price without Jay's approval.

**Exclude:** implementation, migration, paid vendors and integrations, full inventory cleaning, legal/specialist advice, formal valuation work, AVM, index, document portal, native apps, open marketplace and payments.

**If pressed:** reduce workshop/prototype breadth before reducing the core audit and decision pack; do not perform a data audit or prototype free; do not quote a full build before discovery. The prior KES 2.8M–4.5M broad greenfield scenario is retired as a discussion anchor. The technical review supports a smaller Corviat-based KV layer, but its price follows the compatibility and data review.

## Corviat reuse position

Present this as a lower-risk path, not a claim that KV's product already exists. Corviat supplies the reusable operating foundation: tenancy, staff authentication, audit, notifications and workflow/SLA capabilities. KV needs a thin real-estate-specific layer for inventory, public listings and leads. The existing Follow-Up intake is a prototype and will be evaluated, not sold as a finished module.
