# CEO Discovery Meeting Guide

**Audience:** Michael Otieno, CEO, Kenya Valuers & Estate Agents  
**Purpose:** Agree whether to commission a Phase-0 discovery and define the authority needed to complete it.  
**Status:** Internal preparation, not a client commitment

## Meeting outcome to seek

Agree one of three explicit outcomes:

1. **Proceed to Phase 0:** CEO sponsor, named working team, scope, decision cadence and commercial route are confirmed.
2. **Revise the brief:** KV has a different priority or constraint; DesgnPulse returns a revised scope before any commercial commitment.
3. **Pause:** A specific dependency, owner or timing issue prevents discovery; record the owner and revisit date.

The meeting is successful only if it ends with one of these outcomes and a named next action.

## Recommended opening

The earlier proposal identified a meaningful digital gap. We have re-tested the opportunity and narrowed it to the highest-value question: how KV can turn its premium-property and professional-services credibility into more qualified, measurable enquiries. We would like to validate the operating reality with you before committing to a platform scope.

## Questions for Michael

### Business and commercial priorities

- What prompted the call now, and what would make this work worthwhile in the next 12 months?
- Which outcome matters most: higher-quality property enquiries, valuation instructions, commercial opportunities, lower manual effort, or another result?
- Which client segments and areas must win first?
- What makes KV the trusted choice when a prospect can browse a large portal instead?
- Which services or property categories should not be promoted or digitised further?

### Operating reality

- Where do active listings originate, and who decides when they are live, changed, let, sold or withdrawn?
- How are property and valuation enquiries received, assigned and followed up today?
- What response standard can KV realistically own?
- What data, content, media or reporting problems currently consume staff time?
- Who owns final approval for pricing, listing facts, locations, credential claims and service wording?

### Discovery authority

- Will Michael sponsor Phase 0 and appoint an operational product owner?
- Who owns inventory data, technical access, content, lead operations and valuation/compliance review?
- May DesgnPulse inspect a read-only, non-production inventory sample and agreed analytics reports?
- What confidentiality, privacy, procurement or contractual conditions apply before access?
- What budget and sign-off process applies to discovery and a later build?

## Positioning boundaries to state plainly

- Phase 1 is a mobile-first, premium-property and professional-services platform. It is not an open marketplace.
- A web form can request a professional valuation. It cannot generate, imply or certify a formal valuation.
- A public price index, automated value estimate, native app, mortgage marketplace and third-party listings need separate evidence and approval gates.
- A rebuild should not begin until inventory quality, lead workflow, owners, privacy requirements and migration constraints are known.

## Phase-0 offer to discuss

**Objective:** Produce a build-ready decision rather than a speculative feature list.

**Work:**

- Audit an approved non-production inventory sample, media and listing lifecycle.
- Map property, callback and valuation-request lead journeys and ownership.
- Establish analytics, SEO and conversion baselines using approved access only.
- Validate audience priorities and prototype the key mobile and desktop journeys.
- Define privacy, content approval, migration, redirect and acceptance-test requirements.
- Produce architecture options, phased scope, estimate, risks and implementation plan.

**Client inputs:** Named sponsor and working owners, scheduled workshops, approved sample data/analytics, current policies and timely decisions.

**Deliverable:** A signed Phase-1 scope with measurable acceptance criteria, data and integration decisions, a migration approach, commercial estimate, risks and launch plan.
