# Kenya Valuers Sprint 0

## Objective

Convert the meeting PRD into an approved, evidence-based delivery scope without touching client systems or production.

## Setup steps

1. Confirm executive sponsor, product owner, content approver and valuation/compliance owner.
2. Agree the Phase-0 statement of work, project communication and decision cadence.
3. Obtain explicit, read-only approval for only the data and analytics needed for discovery.
4. Select the technical stack and hosting approach after the inventory, lead workflow, privacy and support audit.

## First feature steps

1. Audit a non-production listing export and document the canonical inventory schema.
2. Map enquiry, callback and valuation-request journeys with owners and response expectations.
3. Produce and test mobile and desktop prototypes for discovery, listing detail and service requests.
4. Define migration, redirect, accessibility, privacy and acceptance-test plans.

## Loop candidates

- Expired-listing review and archive workflow.
- Lead assignment and response-SLA reporting.
- Data-quality validation before listing publication.

## Completion criteria

- KV approves priority journeys, data ownership, role model, content policy and Phase-1 scope.
- A sample import passes validation in a non-production environment.
- The selected architecture has a documented cost, security, migration and rollback decision.
- The project has measurable baseline metrics and launch acceptance criteria.
